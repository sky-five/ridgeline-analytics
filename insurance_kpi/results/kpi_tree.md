## What moved

- Auto: loss ratio +5.8 pts: severity +6.9 pts, frequency +0.9 pts, average premium -2.0 pts. Severity rose 24.7%, +/- 7.5% from sampling alone; the simulator planted +15.0%, just outside that range (claim costs are heavy-tailed, and this is one draw). Average earned premium rose 6.5%: the +6.0% rate on policies starting in 2025 is only about half earned in 2025, on top of the 3% yearly trend.
- Home: loss ratio +14.9 pts: frequency +12.7 pts, severity +3.7 pts (it rose 8.3%, +/- 11.0% from sampling; nothing planted), average premium -1.4 pts. By province the frequency jump is in AB: 2.13x its 2024 rate (planted hail: 2.0x); the other provinces are in kpi_segments.csv.
- Expenses: the expense ratio moved -0.2 pts, as the fixed admin cost per policy-year spreads over a higher average premium.
