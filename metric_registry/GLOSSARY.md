# Glossary

Generated by `python -m metric_registry.compile` from [registry.yml](registry.yml). Do not edit.

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| MSRP | The manufacturer's suggested retail price of a vehicle model, fixed for the whole period. |
| Dealer discount | What the dealer takes off MSRP on its own margin, before any incentive program. |
| Incentive program | A manufacturer-funded offer with a type, an eligible segment, a start and end date and an amount per unit. |
| Customer cash | Incentives paid off the price at the point of sale (cash rebates, loyalty cash, the electric-vehicle bonus, quarter-end cash). |
| Financing support | Incentives paid to the lender to lower a loan rate (APR subvention) or a lease payment (lease support). They cost the same as cash but never show in the transaction price. |
| Incentive spend | Customer cash plus financing support. What the programs cost, whichever way they are paid. |
| Transaction price | What the buyer pays for the vehicle, MSRP less the dealer discount and the customer cash. Excludes taxes, fees and trade-ins. |
| Quarter end | The last ten days of a calendar quarter, when a quarter-end cash program runs on every segment. |

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Computed as | Version | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `units_sold` (Units sold) | Vehicles sold, counted on the sale date. One row in the sales table is one vehicle. | `count(sale_id) from fct_sales by sale_date` | 1 | Sales operations |
| `msrp_value` (MSRP value) | MSRP summed over the vehicles sold. | `sum(msrp) from fct_sales by sale_date` | 1 | Sales operations |
| `revenue` (Revenue) | Transaction price summed over the vehicles sold. Financing support is not taken off it, because the buyer never sees it in the price. | `sum(transaction_price) from fct_sales by sale_date` | 1 | Finance |
| `incentive_spend` (Incentive spend) | Customer cash plus financing support on the vehicles sold, counted on the sale date. | `sum(incentive_amount) from fct_sales by sale_date` | 1 | Incentives team |
| `customer_cash` (Customer cash) | The part of incentive spend paid off the price at the point of sale. | `sum(customer_cash_amount) from fct_sales by sale_date` | 1 | Incentives team |
| `incentivised_units` (Incentivised units) | Vehicles sold with at least one incentive program applied. | `sum(case when has_incentive then 1 else 0 end) from fct_sales by sale_date` | 1 | Incentives team |
| `program_spend` (Program spend) | Incentive spend counted per program, so it can be cut by program type. Over all programs it equals incentive spend. | `sum(amount) from fct_incentive_applications by sale_date` | 1 | Incentives team |
| `average_transaction_price` (Average transaction price) | Revenue divided by units sold. | `revenue / units_sold` | 1 | Finance |
| `incentive_per_unit` (Incentive per unit) | Incentive spend divided by units sold, over every vehicle sold, not only the ones with an incentive. | `incentive_spend / units_sold` | 1 | Incentives team |
| `incentive_share_of_msrp` (Incentive share of MSRP) | Incentive spend as a share of MSRP value, the usual way to compare incentive levels across segments with different prices. | `incentive_spend / msrp_value` | 1 | Incentives team |
| `incentive_penetration` (Incentive penetration) | Incentivised units as a share of units sold. | `incentivised_units / units_sold` | 1 | Incentives team |
